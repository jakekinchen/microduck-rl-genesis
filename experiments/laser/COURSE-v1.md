## Frozen visible-development design

actor hashes, course geometry and this specification into each fresh receipt.

laser; 5 seconds target loss/standing. A 4.8 m level route weaves around four

are active MuJoCo colliders. The floor is level: no claim of steps or slopes.

root-position writes, automatic resets or animation substitutions are allowed.

## Independent pass gates

Require full duration, no falls, all six route stations passed in order within
25 cm, final x >=4.35 m, at least 90% visible post-warmup samples within 45 cm
of the moving dot, and no applied robot/obstacle contact over .1 N or penetration
over .1 mm. The final two seconds require speed <=.04 m/s, yaw <=.15 rad/s and
tilt <=15 degrees. Keep the existing gait, posture and self-contact/load gates
unchanged, including bilateral stepping, loaded slip, actual joint-stop
occupancy, 1 mm self-penetration and 200 Hz internal-bracing rejection. Require
all physics-rate samples and original ONNX action bytes, actual torque <= the
retained .6405236195572268 Nm limit, saturation <=2%, actual joint margin >=.02
rad, and moving command-response mean errors <=.05 m/s forward, .05 m/s lateral
and .20 rad/s yaw. Report every case and component separately.

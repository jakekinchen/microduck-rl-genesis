# Slice Brief 004 - M1 closed-loop 14-servo fixture

**Date:** 2026-09-01

## Objective

Retain a deterministic short 14-servo trajectory generated by official MuJoCo
plus the pinned BAM core, then make the Genesis BAM-core profile consume it
under preregistered trajectory tolerances.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Refuse any BAM checkout other than
  `62bd8ce12154340be97e06f7f41a0ca8f116d967`.
- Generate from official MuJoCo at 5 ms for 120 steps with the canonical
  14-joint order, HOME target, fixed 7.35 V, zero randomization, zero bus delay,
  and exact walk model/parameter digests.
- Retain every-step joint positions and base height plus initial conditions,
  source provenance, profile ID, and explicit tolerances.
- Run Genesis under the BAM-core quadratic profile from the same state and
  compare the full trajectory. Preserve negative per-joint/per-time evidence;
  do not reduce the gate to one final scalar.
- Bind the trajectory fixture from the BAM actuator lock and run it in
  `tests/run_all.py`.
- Keep this as BAM-core/MuJoCo-versus-Genesis evidence. Official deployed-mjlab
  consumption remains a later slice and must use its explicit no-gate profile.

## Evidence Boundary

Thresholded agreement over this deterministic standing trajectory validates a
narrow closed-loop BAM/model case. It does not establish official mjlab parity,
walking, backflip, task success, held-out evaluation, or physical authority.

## Stop Conditions

- The pinned reference cannot reproduce byte-identically.
- The source model/parameter digests differ from the open-loop authority lock.
- Closing the tolerance would require assisting or altering either backend.
